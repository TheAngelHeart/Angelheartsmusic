Game Rules

  • The goal is to be the first to get rid of all your cards.
  • Each player is dealt 5 cards to start.
  • On your turn, draw one card from either the main deck or the discard pile.
  • If the card you drew matches a card in your hand (same suit and value), you can discard both. This ends your turn.
  • If there is no match, you must discard any single card from your hand to end your turn.
  • When the main deck runs out, the discard pile is shuffled and becomes the new deck.
  • The game ends when a player has no cards left in their hand.

Special Card Perks & Punishments

  • Pair of Aces: You get to steal one card from your opponent's hand.
  • Pair of Queen of Hearts: You instantly win the game!
  • Pair of Jokers: You get to draw another card and take an extra turn.
  • Pair of Queen of Spades: You must draw two cards from the deck.
  • Pair of Queen of Diamonds: You can discard an extra card.
  • Pair of Queen of Clubs: You lose your next turn.
